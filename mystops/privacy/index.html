My Stops privacy policy

Effective October 3, 2026. This policy covers My Stops, the route planner app from Cajun App Factory.

Location

With your permission, My Stops uses your location to show where you are, order your stops, and guide the drive. If you use directions inside the app, your location is sent to Google to provide that guidance. We do not sell location, and we do not use it for ads.

Camera and photos

With your permission, the app uses the camera or a photo you choose to read an address from a label or a list. On a My Stops Pro subscription, you can also take a photo at a stop as proof of delivery. Photos are used for the route or the proof. They are not used for ads.

Addresses

Stop addresses are sent to Google to place them on the map and to plan the drive. If you choose Google Maps or Waze in Settings, the next stop is opened in that app. That app handles the address under its own privacy policy.

Your Google account

You can use the free app without signing in. My Stops Pro uses the Google account already on your phone to save routes, a mileage log, and proof photos, so they can be restored on another phone. We do not ask you to create a separate password.

Subscription

My Stops Pro is a monthly subscription billed by Google Play. We never see your card number. Google tells us whether the subscription is active so the app can unlock Pro. You cancel in Google Play.

What we do not do

My Stops has no ads. We do not sell your information. A route that you have not saved stays on your phone unless you export or share it.

Contact and deletion

Email support@cajunappfactory.com with a question, or to ask us to delete a saved route, a proof photo, or a mileage record. Uninstalling the app removes routes stored only on the phone. Canceling Pro is done in Google Play.
