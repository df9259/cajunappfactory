My Stops privacy policy

Effective October 3, 2026. This policy covers the My Stops Android app, package com.cajunappfactory.mystops, published by Cajun App Factory.

Location

The app reads precise location, with your permission, to place you on the map and order stops from where you are. Location is used for that route. It is not sold and not used for ads.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera, or a photo you pick, to read a label or a written list of stops. The image is used to extract addresses for the route. It is not used for advertising.

Addresses sent to Google

Stop addresses are sent to Google to place them on the map and to ask for a driving order. Opening a stop also hands that address to Google Maps for turn-by-turn directions. Google's handling of that data is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Google account

Pro backup and restore use the Google account already on the phone. The app receives the account id and email from Google sign-in and stores saved routes under that account. Free use does not require sign-in. There is no separate password.

Subscription

Pro is a monthly subscription sold by Google Play on that same Google account. The app does not collect your card number. It receives a purchase token from Play and sends that token to a Cajun App Factory service, which asks Google whether the subscription is active. The token is kept only long enough to confirm the subscription. Canceling is done in Play.

What we do not do

The app has no ads and no password account. Free routes stay on the device unless you export or share them yourself. We do not sell personal information.

Contact and deletion

Email support@cajunappfactory.com to ask a question or request deletion of a saved route or a purchase token you sent us. Uninstalling the app removes the routes stored on the phone. Canceling the subscription is done in Google Play.
