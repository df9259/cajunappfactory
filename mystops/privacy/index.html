MyStops privacy policy

Effective October 3, 2026. This privacy policy covers MyStops, the route planner app from Cajun App Factory. Questions about this policy go to support@cajunappfactory.com.

Information the app uses

With your permission, MyStops uses your location to show where you are, order your stops, and guide the drive. With your permission, it uses the camera or a photo you choose to read an address from a label or a list. A proof photo you take stays on your phone with that stop. Stop addresses are used to build the route. If you subscribe, Google Play tells the app whether MyStops Pro is active. The app does not ask for your contacts, microphone, card number, or device identifiers such as an IMEI.

Who it is shared with

Stop addresses and, during directions inside the app, your location are sent to Google to place the stops on the map and guide the drive. If you choose Google Maps or Waze in Settings, the next stop opens in that app, which then handles the address under its own policy. Google Play handles the subscription and does not give us your card number. We do not sell this information, and we do not share it with advertisers. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or as part of a sale of the app, and we will say so if that happens.

How it is handled

Location and camera access are asked in the app when you use those features, and you can deny them. The app tells you why before it asks. The connection to Google is encrypted. We do not keep a copy of your route, photos, or mileage. They stay on your phone unless you export or share them yourself.

How long it is kept

We do not keep your routes, photos, or mileage records. Uninstalling MyStops removes the copy on your phone. There is no account to delete. MyStops Pro is canceled in Google Play, and Google keeps the subscription record under its own policy. Email you send to support is kept so we can answer it and refer back to it. You can ask us to delete that email.
