My Stops privacy policy

Effective October 3, 2026. This policy covers My Stops, the route planner app from Cajun App Factory.

Location

With your permission, My Stops uses your location to show where you are, order your stops, and guide the drive. If you use directions inside the app, your location is sent to Google to provide that guidance. We do not sell location, and we do not use it for ads.

Camera and photos

With your permission, the app uses the camera or a photo you choose to read an address from a label or a list. A proof photo stays on your phone with that stop. Photos are not used for ads.

Addresses

Stop addresses are sent to Google to place them on the map and to plan the drive. If you choose Google Maps or Waze in Settings, the next stop is opened in that app. That app handles the address under its own privacy policy. We do not keep a copy of the address.

Your Google account

You can use My Stops without signing in. A Pro subscription uses the Google account already on your phone so Google Play can bill that account. We do not ask you to create a password, and we do not keep a profile.

Subscription

My Stops Pro is a monthly subscription billed by Google Play. We never see your card number. Google tells us whether the subscription is active so the app can unlock Pro. You cancel in Google Play.

What we do not do

My Stops has no ads. We do not sell your information. We do not keep your routes, photos, or mileage records. They stay on your phone unless you export or share them.

Contact

Email support@cajunappfactory.com with a question. Uninstalling the app removes the routes, photos, and mileage stored on the phone. Canceling Pro is done in Google Play.
