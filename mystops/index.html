My Stops

A route planner for a day of stops. Add the addresses, put them in the order you want, and open the next one in Google Maps.

What it does

  • Builds a list of stops by typing, pasting, or a photo of a label.
  • Lets you drag the order. Optimize is a button, not a lock.
  • Hands the next stop to Google Maps for the drive.
  • Saves the list so you can run it again tomorrow.
