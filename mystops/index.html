MyStops · Route planner for package drivers

Scan the list. Drive the next stop.

MyStops turns a label, a photo, a typed list, or a spreadsheet into the day’s route, then opens the next stop in Google Maps or Waze.

Coming soon on Google Play

Free to start. 15 stops.

How it works

Three steps, from a pile of packages to the first stop.

  1. Add the stops

    Scan a label, snap the list, type them in, or import a spreadsheet.

  2. Set the order

    The route follows the roads, or you move a stop and leave it there.

  3. Drive

    Open the next stop in Google Maps or Waze.

Made for a day of drops

The small things that matter when the van is full.

Label scan

Photo in, address out

Point the camera at a package label or a printed list and the addresses become stops. You can also search for an address.

Spreadsheet

Bring the list you have

Import a spreadsheet and it becomes the day’s stops.

Road-aware route

The order follows the roads

Hit Optimize and the stops are ordered along the roads.

Reorder and pin

Move a stop, leave it there

Drag a stop to reorder it, or pin it so it stays put when you hit Optimize.

Proof of delivery

Photo, note, timestamp

Take a proof photo and add a note, with a timestamp. It is stored on your phone.

Daily miles

Know the day’s miles

Today’s miles show on the route, with a daily mileage report.
