How it works

Three steps, from a pile of packages to the first stop.

  1. Scan or add

    Photograph a package label, or type or paste the address. Stops land in your list.

  2. Order

    Tap Optimize for a suggested order, then drag any stop where you want it. Drag-to-pin keeps it there.

  3. Go

    Hand the next stop to Google Maps or Waze, whichever you drive with.
