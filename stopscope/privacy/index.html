StopScope privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026. This privacy policy covers StopScope, the route planner app from Cajun App Factory. The app was called MyStops before this rename. Questions about this policy go to support@cajunappfactory.com.

Information the app uses

With your permission, StopScope uses your location to show where you are, order your stops, and show miles and time left on the route. With your permission, it uses the camera or a photo you choose to read an address from a label or a list. A proof photo you take stays on your phone with that stop. Stop addresses are used to build the route. The app does not ask for your contacts, card number, or device identifiers such as an IMEI.

Who it is shared with

Stop addresses and, when the app places stops or draws the road line, your location are sent to Google (Maps SDK for Android, Geocoding, Places, Routes, and Geolocation). We do not keep a copy of those requests. If you choose Google Maps or Waze, the next stop opens in that app, which then handles the address under its own policy. We do not sell this information, and we do not share it with advertisers. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or as part of a sale of the app, and we will say so if that happens.

How it is handled

Location and camera access are asked in the app when you use those features, and you can deny them. The app tells you why before it asks. The connection to Google is encrypted. We do not run our own server for your routes, photos, or mileage. There is no account with us.

How long it is kept

We do not keep your routes, photos, or mileage records. The copy on your phone is removed when you uninstall StopScope. There is no account with us to delete. Email you send to support is kept so we can answer it and refer back to it. You can ask us to delete that email.

Cajun App Factory. Email support@cajunappfactory.com.
